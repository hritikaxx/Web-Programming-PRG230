Paws

Welcome to Paws, a non-profit pet adoption center dedicated to rescuing, caring for, and rehoming abandoned and rescued animals. We believe every pet deserves a safe, loving, and permanent home.

About Us

Paws Pet Adoption Center works closely with veterinarians and animal welfare volunteers to ensure rescued pets receive proper medical care, nutrition, and emotional support before adoption. Our team focuses on responsible and ethical adoption practices.

Adoption Process

The adoption process at Paws is simple and transparent. Interested adopters can visit our center, meet the pets, and complete a short adoption form. Our team provides guidance to ensure a good match between the pet and owner.

Why Adopt From Paws?

  • All pets are fully vaccinated
  • Medical history is provided
  • Safe and caring shelter environment
  • Post-adoption support available
